### Building Pool Information

All buildings in the pool have been renamed for consistency and clarity.

Each name follows a standardized schema, including the building type and the title:  
For example, **‘ca24’** represents the **Community Architect 2024** title.

Due to time constraints, size categories (e.g., 'small', 'medium', 'large') weren’t added to every building, but most are marked with a cardinal direction (north, south, east, west) and numbered sequentially within their category.

At the end of each building’s name, you’ll find the creator's name, so you can easily identify who contributed each piece! 🎨

This naming structure ensures buildings are easily searchable and identifiable for future use.

We hope this organization makes your mapping process smoother and helps you find the perfect buildings for your next project. Enjoy building and thank you for being part of this event!
