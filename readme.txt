# Community Building Pool

As a community, we’ve come together to create a shared building pool where every member can contribute their unique masterpieces. 

This collaborative effort offers new mappers a warm welcome and a helpful starting point—a .zip file filled with incredible buildings, all crafted by many creative minds.

Each of us plays a part, like individual puzzle pieces—each unique in character and skill. 

But together, we form the bigger picture: **Community**, where strength lies in unity.

**Building Pool Information:**

All the buildings in the pool have been renamed for consistency and clarity.

Each name follows a standardized schema, which includes the building type and the title:

For example, **‘ca24’** represents the **Community Architect 2024** title.  

Due to time constraints, I wasn’t able to add size categories (e.g., 'small', 'medium', 'large') to every building, but most of the buildings have been marked with a cardinal direction (north, south, east, west) and are numbered sequentially within their category.

At the end of each building’s name, you’ll find the creator's name, so you can easily identify who contributed each wonderful piece! 🎨

This naming structure ensures that all the buildings are easily searchable and identifiable for future use. 

We hope this organization makes your mapping process smoother and helps you locate the perfect buildings for your next project. Enjoy building and thank you again for being a part of this event!
